Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Moco Maps ("we", "us") is a social hangout discovery app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how we protect it.

What we collect

  • Email address — used to create and authenticate your account.
  • Username and display name — shown to other users in the app.
  • Hangout data — locations, names, notes, and RSVPs you create.
  • Friend connections — who you add and who adds you.

We do not collect precise GPS location from your device. Hangout locations are placed manually by you on the map.

How we use it

All data is used solely to operate the app — letting you create hangouts, connect with friends, and see events near you. We do not sell your data, run ads, or share it with third parties.

Data storage

Your data is stored on Supabase (supabase.com), a managed Postgres database platform hosted on AWS. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This removes your profile, hangouts, RSVPs, and friend connections immediately.

Children

Moco Maps is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
