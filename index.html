Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

Blazar ("we", "us") operates the Blazar: The Blazer Radar iOS app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Email address — to create and authenticate your account.
  • Username and display name — shown to other users.
  • Profile photo — if you choose to upload one.
  • Event data — locations, names, notes, and RSVPs you create.
  • Friend connections — who you add and who adds you.

We do not collect your device's GPS location. Event locations are placed manually by you on the map.

How we use it

All data is used solely to operate the app. We do not sell your data, run ads, or share it with third parties.

Data storage

Data is stored on Supabase (supabase.com), hosted on AWS. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Account deletion

You can permanently delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account inside the app. This immediately removes your profile, events, RSVPs, and all associated data.

Children

Blazar is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us at mocomapshelp@gmail.com and we will delete it.
